CONTENT DECISIONS TO PRESERVE
History is Activity 6; Dashboard is Activity 7; Design is Activity 8.
The dashboard requires humidity Gauge, float Tile, relay Tile and Pump Auto Mode
Tile. A History graph is optional; an automation list is not required.

Manual disables automatic starts; stopping/protection rules remain active.
Numeric threshold triggers require crossing the threshold.
Use Traces and notifications to identify which fail-safe acted.

Communication options require site testing; no fixed range/lifetime guarantees.

from loss of LAN, Home Assistant or power.
Energy calculations use explicit classroom assumptions and do not constitute
final installation sizing. Account for startup demand, losses and backup needs.
