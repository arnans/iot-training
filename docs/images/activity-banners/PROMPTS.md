## activity-01-sensors.png

Use case: illustration-story. Create one polished decorative activity banner for an adult Thai smart-farming workbook. Flat editorial illustration, clean charcoal outlines, muted sage greens, warm cream #fff6e3, amber highlights, subtle shadows, cohesive restrained palette. Represent an actual farm APPLICATION, not a workshop experiment or technical instruction. No circuit boards, breadboards, wiring diagrams, instructional arrows, icons floating in air, words, letters, numbers, logos or watermark. Wide shallow panoramic scene with one clear visual focus. Requested canvas 1800x300, aspect 6:1. If canvas must be 3:1, compose the ENTIRE farm scene as a continuous narrow horizontal band within the central 50% of image height; top and bottom quarters plain solid warm cream. All people and greenhouse roofs must fit inside that central band. Spread scenery across the whole width. Distant panoramic viewpoint, small complete human figures, simple shapes recognizable at shallow banner size. Scene: A farmer walking through a greenhouse, checking growing conditions among healthy plants. Sunlight, shade, and open vents suggest the conditions being observed.
