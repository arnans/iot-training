1. Board firmware — six images

One image per board type, per zone colour. The zone colour is baked in: a red-zone image only joins the red SSID. Built and verified 12 Sep 2026.

Board Red zone Green zone Blue zone
GoGo-IoT sensor board (ESP32-C3) gogo-iot-red.factory.bin gogo-iot-green.factory.bin gogo-iot-blue.factory.bin
Relay board (ESP32-C6-Relay_X1) gogo-relay-red.factory.bin gogo-relay-green.factory.bin gogo-relay-blue.factory.bin

To flash a board: plug it into a laptop over USB-C, open https://web.esphome.io in Chrome or Edge → Connect → pick the serial port → Install → choose the .bin for that board and zone. Needs internet.

The colour on the board must match the colour on the kit and on the team card. A mismatched board joins the wrong WiFi and the team's Home Assistant never sees it. The board says which it is — its name reads gogo-iot-red-a1b2c3.

Spare boards flashed during the workshop must be flashed to that table's zone colour.

Instructor kit — a spare laptop mirroring a team kit, projected. It also rescues a team whose laptop dies. Three things teams don't have: the Auto / Off / Manual pump control box for the Session 2 debrief, a Home Assistant Cloud account for the Session 3 remote-access demo, and a saved morning history graph for teams that shut their VM down at lunch.

Kit prep, the two that fail silently: flip the float cylinder on its rod so it closes when wet (otherwise a cut wire reads "water present" and a pump can run dry), and colour-code board, kit and team card as one matching set.
