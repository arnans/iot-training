Activity 4: each of 4.1–4.8 needs a clear screen cue. Measure chatter; explain
hysteresis and retaining state between thresholds; distinguish trigger/condition/
action; predict darkness-before-time and add time trigger; notification;
safe lamp-to-pump changeover; humidity control; Auto/Manual helper.

Activity 5: introduce simulate → observe → fix → repeat. Then 5.1 low water;
5.2 commanded ON without vibration (15 s); 5.3 unavailable/unknown sensor;
5.4 run-time cutoff (30 s classroom setting); 5.5 maintenance and controller

ON command does not prove pump operation; vibration does not prove water flow.
